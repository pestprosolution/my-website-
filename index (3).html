About Pest Pro Solution

A Vadodara Company That Answers the Phone

"No query is too small, and no pest issue too minor" — that's not a tagline we picked for effect, it's genuinely how we schedule our week.

PEST PRO SOLUTION is a Government Licensed Pest Control Company based right here in Vadodara, Gujarat. We provide reliable, safe and effective pest control for Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Pharmaceutical, Food Processing, Hospitality, Healthcare, Educational and Institutional facilities — and we've built our name on Quality, Integrity, Safety and Operational Excellence rather than on the loudest advertising.

With experienced technicians, modern pest management techniques and environmentally responsible treatment methods, we help protect your property from termites, rodents, cockroaches, ants, mosquitoes, flies, bed bugs, birds, stored product insects and other public health pests. Regulatory Compliance isn't an afterthought here — it's the standard every job is measured against, which is exactly why our documentation holds up under audit.

Whether you need a one-time treatment or a comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC), we deliver customized, Long-Term Protection built around Scientific Pest Management and Sustainable Solutions — keeping your premises clean, safe and genuinely pest-free, visit after visit.
